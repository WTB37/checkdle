How to play

Check out the day's score in nine darts or fewer. Fewest darts wins.

  1. 1Hold, aim, let go. Touch the board and slide your finger. A magnifier shows where the dart is headed. Let go to throw. On a computer, point and click.
  2. 2The crosshair sways. It wanders around your finger or cursor. Time your release.
  3. 3Darts scatter. Each dart lands somewhere near the crosshair, with fresh luck every throw. Big singles are safe. Trebles, doubles and the bull often slip into the next bed.
  4. 4Your score left stays hidden. Keep count in your head.
  5. 5Three darts a visit, three visits. Go below zero, leave 1, or reach zero without a double and it's bust: back to your score from the start of that visit.
